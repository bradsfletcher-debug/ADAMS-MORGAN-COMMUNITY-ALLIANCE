About Our Organization

About ADAMS MORGAN COMMUNITY ALLIANCE

A Washington, DC nonprofit organization dedicated to collaborative stewardship, civic engagement, and long-term neighborhood well-being across Adams Morgan.

Our Story & Civic Purpose

Adams Morgan has long stood as one of Washington, DC's most vibrant, historic, and culturally diverse neighborhoods—defined by its iconic 19th-century rowhouses, tree-lined residential blocks, independent storefronts, and rich multicultural heritage. ADAMS MORGAN COMMUNITY ALLIANCE was established to unite neighbors around the shared responsibility of preserving and strengthening this unique community fabric.

Operating as a tax-exempt 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization (Federal EIN: 82-1834452), we serve as a civic bridge between long-time residents, newcomers, local merchants, youth, seniors, and public-interest stakeholders. We believe that thriving neighborhoods are built when community members have transparent avenues to collaborate on practical local improvements.

Our Vision & Core Values

Inclusive Representation

Ensuring neighbors of every background, age, and tenure feel heard, respected, and welcomed in neighborhood decisions.

Transparent Stewardship

Maintaining clear public accountability, ethical governance, and responsible management of charitable resources.

Neighborhood Resilience

Supporting small businesses, green infrastructure, and neighbor-to-neighbor mutual aid networks.

Constructive Partnership

Working alongside local civic bodies, District agencies, and peer nonprofits to solve shared urban challenges.
